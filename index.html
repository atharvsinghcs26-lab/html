Why two kitchens under one roof

Kora started as a three-table coffee counter. People kept asking for something to eat with their chai, so we added a clay oven and a small dinner menu. We still bake, roast and grind everything on the premises, and we buy vegetables and dairy from farms within 60 km.

Come alone with a book, or bring the whole family. Both work here.
